# Betelgeuse

*Betelgeuse · α Ori · Orión*

Supergigante roja en el hombro de Orión. Si estuviera en el lugar del Sol, llegaría más allá de la órbita de Marte.

## English
A red supergiant on Orion's shoulder. Placed where the Sun is, it would reach beyond the orbit of Mars.

*It will end as a supernova, perhaps within about 100,000 years. In late 2019 it visibly dimmed behind its own dust cloud.*
