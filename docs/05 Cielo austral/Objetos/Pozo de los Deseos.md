# Pozo de los Deseos

*Wishing Well Cluster · NGC 3532 · Cúmulo abierto*

Cúmulo muy rico cerca de la nebulosa de Carina. Con telescopio parecen monedas brillando en el fondo de un pozo.

## English
A very rich cluster near the Carina Nebula. In a telescope it looks like coins glinting at the bottom of a well.

*It was the first target observed by the Hubble Space Telescope, in 1990.*
