# Fomalhaut

*Fomalhaut · α PsA · Pez Austral*

La «estrella solitaria» de primavera: brilla sola en una zona con pocas estrellas. Está rodeada por un enorme anillo de polvo.

## English
The "lonely star" of spring: it shines alone in a sparse region. It is surrounded by a huge ring of dust.

*Its debris ring was imaged in great detail by the James Webb telescope.*
