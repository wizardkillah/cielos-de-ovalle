# Capella

*Capella · α Aur · Cochero*

Sistema de cuatro estrellas que a simple vista parece una sola, amarilla. Desde aquí apenas se asoma sobre el horizonte norte.

## English
A system of four stars that looks like a single yellow one. From here it barely clears the northern horizon.

*Its two main giants orbit each other every 104 days.*
